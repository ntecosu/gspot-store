How long it takes

Pieces ship from different partners, so one order can arrive in more than one parcel.

ItemMade / packedDelivery after dispatch
Dice Cap (made to order)2–7 business daysEU 3–8, UK and US 4–10, rest of the world 5–15 business days

These are estimates, not guarantees. Holidays, customs and carrier delays can add time. If your order hasn't arrived within 30 business days of dispatch, email us and we'll sort it out.

Customs, duties and taxes

Inside the EU there are no extra charges on delivery. Outside the EU (including the UK, US and Switzerland), your country may charge import duties or taxes on arrival. Those are set by your country, aren't included in our prices, and are paid by the recipient.

Address problems

Check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can change it until the order has been dispatched. If a parcel comes back to us because of a wrong or incomplete address, we'll re-send it once you pay the new shipping cost.

Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 15 business days, or your parcel arrives damaged, email support@gspot.world with your order number (and photos if it's damaged). We'll send a replacement or refund you.
